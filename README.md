## The entry

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes a single-line roast of my fridge to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My fridge hums a lullaby every night to an audience of one expired yogurt and a jar of pickles that has outlived two of my relationships.

The joke lands on a specific, recognisable detail — the forgotten condiments at the back of the fridge — and turns on a twist in the last few words: the pickles have outlasted my love life.
